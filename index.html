Six round-trip options for Mom & Dad, Sunday to Sunday, with no more than one stop each way.

The short version

Every price on this page is the round-trip total for both parents (2 travelers), not a per-person fare. A smaller “approx. per person” figure on each card is simply half of that total.

These are round-trip flights from Atlanta (ATL) to Reno (RNO) on Sunday, December 20 out and Sunday, December 27 back. Every price is the round-trip total for both parents (two adults) in economy, not the cost for one person, and does not include checked bags.

Southwest Basic is cheaper, but seat selection costs extra. Choice includes seat selection, so you can sit together. On the Southwest options below, Choice is $1,535 total for both and Basic is $1,335 total for both.

Top recommendation: Option 1 — Southwest via Nashville, $1,535 total for both on Choice. It is the cheapest way to sit together, Nashville is basically on the way, and it lands in Reno earliest, at 2:30 PM.
